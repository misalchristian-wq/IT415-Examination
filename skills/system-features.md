# System features

## Required (Section V - all 12)
1. Show at least 5 products with names and prices (₱)
2. Add to cart with on-screen buttons
3. Increase / decrease quantity in the cart
4. Remove a product from the cart
5. Auto-compute each subtotal and the overall total on every change
6. Clear order summary of all cart items
7. Cash payment through a numeric input
8. Validate: reject blank, non-numeric, negative, insufficient, each with a clear message
9. Change = Amount Paid - Total Amount
10. Clear payment confirmation after success
11. Digital receipt: items, quantities, subtotals, total, amount paid, change, transaction reference
12. New transaction clears cart, payment amount, and receipt

## Allowed simplifications (Section VII)
Hard-coded products and prices, no database (memory is fine), any language covered in class.

## Out of scope (do not build)
Login/authentication, inventory, real payment gateway, QR or card payment, deployment, product add/edit. Anything not in Section V is optional and earns no extra credit.

## Optional small extras (only after all 12 work)
Keyboard Enter to pay (included), a "Clear cart" button, a printable receipt style.
